## When you need a commercial license

You need a commercial license whenever Kestrix is used for a commercial

In practice that means running Kestrix inside a for-profit company or as part
of a paid product or service, including internal build and deployment
pipelines that support commercial activity.

evaluation, private study, hobby work, academic research, and use by
charitable, educational, public research, public safety, health,
environmental, or government organizations.

## What a commercial license grants

A commercial license grants the right to use Kestrix for a commercial purpose,
under the plan matching the total number of user accounts across every
instance you run. The plans, the account-counting rule, and the prices are in
[PRICING.md](PRICING.md). All other terms of the
[PolyForm Noncommercial License](LICENSE) — the notice requirement, the patent
license and its defense clause, the absence of any warranty, and the
disclaimer of liability — continue to apply unchanged.

A commercial license does not grant the right to sublicense, resell, or
redistribute Kestrix as a competing product or hosted service. Those
arrangements need a separate written agreement.

## No technical enforcement

Kestrix contains no license key, activation check, account limit, or telemetry.
Nothing in the software enforces a plan or reports usage. Compliance is a
contractual matter, not a technical one.

# Kestrix Pricing

Kestrix is free for personal, non-commercial use under the
[PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE) — see that file for the exact
terms. Commercial use requires a paid plan and the separate
[commercial license](LICENSE-COMMERCIAL.md), priced by how many people use it,
not by company revenue or headcount.

## How a plan is determined

The unit is **user accounts**, summed across every Kestrix instance your
organization runs. There is no limit on the number of instances you may
run, and instances are not counted or priced separately: only the total
number of accounts is. Ten instances with ten accounts each is 100
accounts — the same as one instance with 100 accounts, and the same even
if some of those accounts belong to the same person.

## Plans

| Plan | Accounts | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Small | 1–3 | $3/month |
| Team | 4–10 | $25/month |
| Scale | 11-20 | $50/month |
| Enterprise | 20+ | Please contact |

Each plan is a flat monthly price for its whole account range, not a
per-seat charge — adding one more account within your current range does
not change what you pay.

## Non-commercial use

Personal projects, evaluation, and learning are free without a paid plan,
regardless of account count. See the [PolyForm Noncommercial License](LICENSE)
for what counts as a noncommercial purpose; it also covers charitable,
educational, public research, public safety, health, environmental, and
government organizations.
